Notice DRD-P01 // Visitor Intake

Intake Privacy

This notice applies only to the visitor-intake form currently shown on Death Row Diner.

What the form asks for

The form can include a name or alias, email address, city/state, optional final-meal response, and optional message. Email is the only required visitor field.

Why it is collected

The current purpose is to keep a record of people who want future information about the developing Death Row Diner concept and to receive messages visitors choose to send.

How submission works

The form is delivered through FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Do not submit sensitive personal information in the message field.

Browser storage

After a valid submission, the site stores only a temporary DRD record number and the submitted name or alias in your browser so the confirmation page can display them. The site does not intentionally store your email address in browser storage.

What this notice does not claim

This page does not invent future restaurant privacy practices, reservation policies, payment rules, or operating policies that have not been established yet.
